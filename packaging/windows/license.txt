3. HARDWARE SAFETY & PERMANENT DATA ERASURE DISCLOSURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Vajra includes industrial-grade data destruction capabilities implementing
NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 1 and IEEE 2883-2022 media sanitization standards.

- DESTRUCTIVE NATURE: Executing sanitization or overwriting routines will
  PERMANENTLY DESTROY all data, partition tables, metadata, and filesystems
  on targeted storage devices. Recovering data after a verified sanitization
  operation is mathematically and physically impossible.
- OPERATOR CONFIRMATION: The software implements a Two-Phase Hardware
  Identity Confirmation Gate requiring manual serial number verification.
  You are solely responsible for confirming that the target device selected
  is the intended target and not an active system or critical storage device.
- DISCLAIMER OF LIABILITY: The developers and contributors disclaim all
  liability for unintentional data loss, hardware damage, or business
  interruption resulting from improper operator target selection or execution.
